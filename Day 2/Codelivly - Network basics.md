## The Big Two: Internet vs. Intranet

The Internet

The **Internet** is the ultimate WAN. It is literally a "network of networks." It is a massive, global, public infrastructure that connects millions of private LANs and WANs together.

- **The Golden Rule:** The Internet is **public**. Anyone with an ISP connection can join it. When you put a server on the internet, the whole world can theoretically talk to it.

The Intranet

An **Intranet** is a **private** network. It uses the exact same technologies as the internet (like TCP/IP and web browsers), but access is strictly restricted to a specific organization or group of people.

## The Middleman: The ISP

If you have a LAN in your house, and the Internet is a global WAN, how do your two worlds connect? You need a bridge.

That bridge is your **ISP (Internet Service Provider)**.
